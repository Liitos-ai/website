Building the intelligence layer for real-world work.

The next frontier of AI is understanding and performing the work that runs the world's organizations. Today, this knowledge is fragmented across systems, conversations, documents, and human decisions. Understanding how work actually happens, why decisions are made, and what outcomes follow remains one of the biggest bottlenecks in bringing AI into the enterprise.

Liitos is building the infrastructure to bridge this gap. We transform real enterprise workflows into high-quality training data, evaluations, and reinforcement learning environments for frontier models. Our broader vision is to build a trusted enterprise ontology that captures how organizations operate, creating a shared intelligence layer that enables frontier labs, forward-deployed teams, and consulting firms to train, evaluate, and deploy AI across enterprises at scale.

Founded by former Meta Superintelligence Labs talent, with experience across post-training, reinforcement learning, and agent infrastructure.

Backed by venture capital and angels across all major frontier AI labs.
